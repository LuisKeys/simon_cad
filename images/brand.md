## Visual Identity (Derived from Image)

### Color Palette
Primary and secondary colors inferred from the image:

- **Primary Orange:** #FF7A00 → Energy, innovation, AI focus
- **Deep Black:** #0A0A0A → Authority, enterprise, seriousness
- **Dark Charcoal:** #1F1F1F → Depth, technical sophistication
- **Glow Orange Accent:** #FFA94D → Highlights, neural/AI glow effects
- **Soft Gray:** #BFBFBF → Supporting text, balance

#### Usage Guidelines
- Use **black/dark backgrounds** as default
- Apply **orange for emphasis**, CTAs, and key phrases (e.g., “AI Brains”)
- Use **glow effects sparingly** to suggest intelligence/AI
- Maintain high contrast for readability

---

### Typography

#### Primary Font (Headlines)
- **Sans-serif, bold, modern** (Recommended: Inter, Montserrat, or Poppins)
- Heavy weights (700–900)
- Tight letter spacing for impact

#### Secondary Font (Body)
- Clean sans-serif (Inter / Open Sans)
- Medium/light weights (400–500)
- Optimized for readability

#### Styling Rules
- Headlines: **ALL CAPS or Title Case**
- Emphasize keywords in **orange**
- Keep lines short and punchy

---

### Visual Style

#### Aesthetic
- Futuristic
- High-tech
- Enterprise-grade
- Cyber/AI-inspired

#### Common Elements
- Neural networks / digital brain visuals
- Data streams, graphs, signals
- City skylines (business context)
- Subtle grid or HUD overlays

#### Lighting Style
- Dark scenes with **orange glow highlights**
- Contrast between shadow and illuminated elements
- “AI core” glowing effect

---

### Imagery Guidelines
- Use **realistic + digital hybrid visuals**
- Prefer **black/orange color grading**
- Include **technology metaphors** (brains, circuits, networks)
- Avoid generic stock imagery

---

### Layout Principles
- Left: messaging (text-heavy)
- Right: personality/face or visual anchor
- Strong horizontal composition (banner-friendly)
- Clear hierarchy:
  1. Headline
  2. Subheadline
  3. Credibility
  4. CTA or value strip
